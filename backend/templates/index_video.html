About This Project

A cutting-edge research project combining artificial intelligence, natural language processing, and medical video analysis to provide accurate, video-supported answers to medical procedure questions.

🎯

Research Focus

This system demonstrates the application of natural language processing and video understanding for medical question answering, utilizing the MedVidQA dataset.

🎬

Video Integration

Each answer is accompanied by relevant video segments from verified medical procedures, providing visual guidance alongside textual explanations.

🤖

Multimodal AI Pipeline

Powered by Llama 4 Scout (vision), Whisper (voice), and Llama 3.3 (language). Smart semantic retrieval ensures accurate, contextually relevant answers.

📊

Dataset Excellence

Built on the comprehensive MedVidQA dataset containing 784 verified medical videos with 2,714 detailed question-answer pairs covering diverse medical procedures and step-by-step guidance.

🥽

AR-Ready Pipeline

Designed for Meta Quest 3 integration with hands-free voice input, real-time camera frame analysis, and step-by-step AR guidance overlays for medical procedures.
